🗑️ Bulk Delete by Range

Delete a range of files from the storage channel AND your drive. This is permanent.

📌 Both links must be from your STORAGE CHANNEL. The bot scans your drive for any files whose message ID falls in this range, then deletes the Telegram messages and removes the entries from the drive.
